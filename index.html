UFA Auction Rules & Squad Limits

1. Room Access

Every room requires an authentic 12-character alphanumeric Room Code and an 8-character alphanumeric Password. Rooms support 4-6 Clubs as chosen by the Host.

2. Squad Caps (Hard Server Enforcement)

  • Managers: Max 1 per club.
  • Forwards: Max 4 per club.
  • Midfielders: Max 3 per club.
  • Defenders: Max 3 per club.
  • Goalkeepers: Max 1 per club.

3. Sequence Flow & Clock

Drafting progresses sequentially through Managers → Midfielders → Forwards → Goalkeepers → Defenders. Each lot has a 12-second bidding timer with a 3-second suspense interval between cards.

The Idea Behind UFA

UFA was created with one simple idea: football transfers are interesting enough to be a game of their own. The excitement isn't only in watching a player score. It's in deciding who to sign, how much to spend, when to bid, and whether to walk away.

The Creator wanted to build something around that part of football — the decisions behind the team. So he built UFA, THE Ultimate Football Auction.

What started as a personal idea became a working auction experience where players and managers can be bought, sold, and fought over through live bidding.

Today, thousands of active users play UFA daily with their friends and mates.
